# Visual Brainstorming Graph

A branching visual brainstorming tool where AI-generated images become reusable ideas: explore alternatives, mark what works, and combine the best parts into new directions without losing your creative history.

Instead of a linear prompt history, ideas live on an interactive graph:

- **Concept nodes** hold a human-readable creative direction. Refine them through chat while they're drafts; they freeze when you click Generate.
- **Image nodes** are the candidate images a concept produces. Add feedback and regional annotations to say what works and what doesn't.
- **Branch** from any concept or image to explore a new direction, and **connect multiple parents** to combine branches into a new concept.

**generate → compare → annotate → branch → combine → refine → generate again**

> Local-only proof of concept. No auth, multi-tenancy, or billing.
